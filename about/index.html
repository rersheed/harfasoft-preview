ABOUT HARFASOFT

Technology and talent for a digital Nigeria

HARFASOFT Academy Limited is a Nigerian technology company specialising in software development, IT consultancy, digital transformation, ICT training and enterprise technology solutions.

Our background

HARFASOFT ACADEMY LIMITED was incorporated in Nigeria on 29 September 2022 and is registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), Nigeria under RC number 1982700.

We exist to help organisations — businesses, schools, healthcare providers and government institutions — replace manual, paper-based processes with reliable digital systems, and to equip people with the ICT skills they need to use them well.

Alongside client projects, we develop our own software products: LumiSAS for school management, Alkhairat HMS for hospital management and BizBook for simplified business accounting.
