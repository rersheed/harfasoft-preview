BUSINESS ACCOUNTING APP

BizBook

Simple bookkeeping for growing businesses.

BizBook helps small business owners keep proper books without needing an accountant. Record sales and expenses, keep track of customers who buy on credit and their repayments, and see simple reports that show how the business is really doing. BizBook is available on the web and as an Android app.

What BizBook does

Sales

Record every sale in seconds, cash or credit.

Expenses

Log business expenses so you know where money goes.

Credit tracking

See which customers owe you and how much.

Repayments

Record repayments and keep customer balances up to date.

Reports

Clear summaries of sales, expenses and outstanding credit.
