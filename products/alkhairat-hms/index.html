What Alkhairat HMS does

Patient registration

Create and find patient records quickly with consistent demographic data.

Consultations

Doctors record complaints, diagnoses, prescriptions and requests in one place.

Laboratory

Receive test requests and return results straight to the consulting doctor.

Pharmacy

Dispense prescriptions and keep track of drug stock.

Billing

Accurate bills for consultations, tests and drugs, with payment tracking.

Administration

User roles, departments and reports for hospital management.

Who Alkhairat HMS is for

  • Private hospitals
  • Clinics and medical centres
  • Diagnostic laboratories
  • Doctors, nurses and hospital administrators
