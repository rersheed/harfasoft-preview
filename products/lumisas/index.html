SCHOOL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

LumiSAS

School management, simplified.

LumiSAS brings admissions, attendance, results, fees and parent communication into one platform. It is designed for the realities of Nigerian schools — including conventional schools, Tahfeez (Qur'an memorisation) schools and Majlis institutions — so administrators, teachers and parents all work from the same accurate records.

FEATURES

What LumiSAS does

Admissions

Capture applications and student records digitally, from enquiry to enrolment.

Attendance

Fast daily attendance for classes and halaqahs, with clear history per student.

Results

Record assessments and produce report sheets without manual calculations.

Fees management

Track fees, payments and outstanding balances per student and term.

Parent access

Keep parents informed about attendance, performance and fees.

Tahfeez & Majlis support

Workflows suited to Qur'anic memorisation progress and Islamic learning institutions.

Who LumiSAS is for

  • Nursery, primary and secondary schools
  • Tahfeez (Qur'an memorisation) schools
  • Majlis and Islamic learning institutions
  • School proprietors and administrators
